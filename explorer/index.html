What the labels mean

Machine annotations. OCR can be wrong. Word boxes are approximate guides; confidence repeats an uncalibrated line score. Columns and tables can confuse reading order.

Separate community text. Wikisource proofread/validated labels describe the original community status. That tier has text and source references, without image bytes or Bayanat-verified word boxes.
