# Attribution

**Original scans and cataloging:** Arabic Collections Online, hosted by NYU
Libraries, and the source institution identified in each row. Source providers
include NYU, Princeton, Columbia, Cornell, American University of Beirut,
American University in Cairo and the United Arab Emirates National Archives.
Provider presence in the source catalog is not a promise of inclusion in every
image snapshot. Follow the exact manifest, book and PDF links in the data.

**Community transcriptions:** Arabic Wikisource contributors, credited through
the exact revision and page-history links in `community_text`. File-description
metadata credits the relevant scan contributors and source institutions.

**Reference-pair PDFs and page renders:** the copyright holders, authors and
uploaders named in `metadata/reference-pairs-source-files.jsonl.gz` and the
frozen file-description revisions. Preserve the per-file CC BY-SA 4.0 notices,
source links and permission evidence with derivatives. Bayanat renders the
original PDF pages and associates the existing community body transcription;
it does not claim authorship of either original component.

**Pipeline, page representation and reports:** Bayanat Labs, 2026.

Bayanat did not digitize the original books or perform the original community
proofreading. Its contribution is the acquisition pipeline, normalized visual
page representation, machine OCR with approximate localization, source-version
binding, deduplication and quality reporting.
